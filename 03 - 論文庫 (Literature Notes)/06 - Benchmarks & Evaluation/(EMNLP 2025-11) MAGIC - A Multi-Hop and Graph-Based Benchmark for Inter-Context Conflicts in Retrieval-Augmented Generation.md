# MAGIC: A Multi-Hop and Graph-Based Benchmark for Inter-Context Conflicts in Retrieval-Augmented Generation

## 一話摘要
利用 knowledge-graph-based construction 產生可解釋、細微且多樣的 **inter-context conflicts**，特別檢驗 multi-hop reasoning 下模型能否偵測並定位 retrieved contexts 之間的矛盾。
