# MultiDocFusion

MultiDocFusion combines visual region detection, OCR, document-hierarchy reconstruction and DFS-based hierarchical chunk construction for long industrial documents.

## Taxonomy decision
**Primary D02 / Secondary D01**. The central problem is structure-aware chunking; document parsing supplies the source hierarchy.

## Reported result
The official abstract reports 8–15% retrieval-precision improvement and 2–3% ANLS QA improvement over its baselines.
