# Intelligent Document Parsing (IDP)

IDP addresses end-to-end parsing of pixel-based and scanned documents, targeting semantic fragmentation and error propagation from separated layout/OCR pipelines.

## Taxonomy decision
**Primary D01**. The scientific intervention is source-document parsing and layout grounding, not retrieval-unit selection or query-time retrieval.
